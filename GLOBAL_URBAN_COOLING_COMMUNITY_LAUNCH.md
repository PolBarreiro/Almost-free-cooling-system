# Global Urban Cooling Implementation Manual — volunteer launch desk

**Status: public invitation / founding phase. This is not yet the manual and is not an endorsed scientific resource.**

We are seeking **human** scientists, engineers, city staff, urban planners, public-health experts, cost analysts, translators, and documentation maintainers to establish an independent, free, openly licensed, community-maintained practical manual for governments.

**Start here:** [Introduce yourself and volunteer](https://github.com/PolBarreiro/Almost-free-cooling-system/issues/1) · [Help establish the independent repository](https://github.com/PolBarreiro/Almost-free-cooling-system/issues/2).

## What the community will produce
A continuously maintained, location-specific **how-to** reference: identify climate and urban conditions; choose feasible existing interventions; plan permits, procurement and installation; estimate capital/operating/maintenance costs and possible revenue; monitor heat exposure and outcomes; and update guidance when evidence changes. The project is about implementation, not unmoderated technology debates.

## Founding tasks (human volunteers)
- Select and create an **independent public repository** with an explicit permissive documentation license (e.g. CC BY 4.0 for text, subject to review) and a code license if software is added.
- Draft and approve README, CONTRIBUTING, CODE_OF_CONDUCT, GOVERNANCE, review policy and issue/PR templates.
- Map relevant UNEP, World Bank, C40 and WRI material before commissioning overlapping content; record links, licenses, scope, gaps and update dates.
- Set up chapter templates for climate/location decision tables, documented case studies, transparent life-cycle cost/revenue models, safety and accessibility.
- Recruit at least two consenting human maintainers from different disciplines and establish a documented review/merge process.

## Proposed acceptance rules (to be ratified by humans)
Every implementation recommendation should identify: location and climate conditions; target users; baseline and comparison; metric and measurement method (air temperature, mean radiant temperature, heat exposure etc. as appropriate); source and date; evidence quality and uncertainty; costs and currency/year; maintenance, water/energy needs; safety, equity and permitting; conditions where it should **not** be used. Clearly separate observed results from simulations and hypotheses. No unsupported quantitative claims.

AI may assist a contributor but is **not** an author, scientific reviewer, or maintainer. The submitting human must verify citations, calculations, permission to reuse materials, and factual claims. A different human reviewer should approve technical contributions.

## Volunteer onboarding
Comment on [the recruitment issue](https://github.com/PolBarreiro/Almost-free-cooling-system/issues/1) with your public expertise and one bounded task you can own. Do not share personal contact information. No affiliation or endorsement will be attributed without consent. Once a separate repository exists, all new work should move there.

## Important separation
This file is a **temporary public signpost** hosted in an existing experimental cooling-system repository. It does **not** endorse that repository's technical claims, and its current license/reuse terms must not be assumed to apply to the future manual. The future manual needs its own explicit open license and independent human governance.
