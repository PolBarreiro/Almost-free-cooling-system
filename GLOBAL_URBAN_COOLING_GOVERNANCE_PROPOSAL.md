# Global Urban Cooling Manual — proposed human governance and review rules

**Status: founding proposal, not ratified.** This is a temporary working document in an experimental repository, not a license declaration or a claim of scientific endorsement. Human volunteers must ratify these rules in a separate public manual repository.

## Ownership and maintainers
- Humans alone author, verify, approve, and maintain the manual. AI is optional drafting or research assistance; contributors remain accountable for every claim and citation.
- Recruit at least two consenting, publicly identifiable GitHub maintainers with complementary expertise (ideally engineering/climate science and urban planning/municipal implementation). Do not name or assign anyone without explicit consent.
- Record maintainers, responsibilities, conflicts of interest, review availability, and succession procedure in the future repository. No affiliation implies institutional endorsement.
- Maintainers may step down at any time; nominate replacements openly and transfer access before departure.

## Contributions
Open an issue describing the place, climate, budget, intended audience, implementation question, and proposed human author/reviewer. Contributions should be bounded, actionable and reusable.

Each practical chapter or case study must document:
1. Local climate, urban form, target population, constraints, baseline and alternatives.
2. Existing intervention, materials, implementation steps, permits, procurement and responsible parties.
3. Capital and recurring costs (currency, price year, assumptions), maintenance, energy and water demand.
4. Safety, accessibility, environmental and equity tradeoffs; contraindications and failure modes.
5. Source-backed measured outcomes, methods and dates; distinguish field measurements, models and unverified hypotheses.
6. Evidence quality, uncertainty, applicability limits, source licenses, and a next-review date.

## Review and publication
- All substantive technical changes require review by **at least one independent human** with relevant expertise, separate from the author. High-risk safety/health or large-cost recommendations should receive a second qualified review.
- Reviewers verify cited sources, calculations, real-world applicability, permissions and safety limitations; request corrections before approval.
- No contributor may self-approve a technical chapter. Document decisions in public issues or pull requests.
- Mark unreviewed drafts clearly and do not present them as government-ready guidance.
- Fix safety-critical errors promptly; record corrections and change history. Revisit chapters when new measurements, standards, or local conditions change.

## Conduct and transparent participation
Be respectful, specific and evidence-focused. No harassment, discriminatory abuse, undisclosed promotion, fabricated data, or unsolicited mass outreach. Disclose relevant financial or institutional interests. Never publish private contact information without permission.

## Founding decisions still needed
- Create a **separate public repository** and select explicit open documentation and software licenses after checking source compatibility.
- Ratify governance, a code of conduct, contribution guidelines, chapter and issue templates, and repository permissions.
- Invite scientists, engineers, planners and municipal practitioners via approved community channels; obtain approval before posting to external groups.
- Publish a named maintainer roster only after individual consent. Track accepted pull requests, independent reviews and maintenance activity with public links.
- Once at least two human maintainers are actively reviewing and contributors are delivering accepted source-backed material, reduce or pause automated recruitment.
